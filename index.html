01 / MISSION

We exist to move an idea from conviction to evidence.

The Graham Foundation is an independent research initiative. We define emerging questions, build open test beds, publish what happened, and preserve the path from hypothesis to result.

We do not ask whether an idea sounds conventional. We ask whether it can be stated precisely, challenged publicly, and tested repeatedly.

Our missionBuild a durable public home for experimental technology, where claims remain inspectable and failure remains evidence.

M/36 — PROTOCOL RESEARCHv0.1 DRAFT

Can agents communicate in 36 characters?

Morse/36 investigates whether agents sharing a deterministic registry can exchange compact, auditable instructions instead of repeatedly transmitting verbose task payloads.

THE TEST

Measure semantic accuracy, wire cost, latency, registry drift, replay resistance, and failure recovery against JSON and binary baselines.

03 / OPERATING PRINCIPLES

The work is public. The standard is evidence.

01

Atomic claims

State one claim clearly enough that it can be tested, disproved, or revised without moving the goalposts.

02

Open inspection

Publish definitions, assumptions, methods, test vectors, and negative results alongside the conclusion.

03

Independent inquiry

Research direction is not subordinate to a product roadmap, popularity contest, or commercial deadline.

04

Reproducible proof

A result becomes useful when another person can inspect the record and attempt the same test.

04 / INDEPENDENCE

Built in public. Governed through the record.

The Graham Foundation operates independently as an open-research initiative. Collaborators may contribute ideas, code, infrastructure, or criticism. No contribution purchases control over a finding.
